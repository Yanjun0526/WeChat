\section{Level 3 Supplementary Outcome Models}
\label{app:c-level-3}

The Level 3 supplementary models use the agent--topic pair as the unit of analysis. Model A uses the continuous matching measure, \varname{MatchScore\_mean}; Model B uses the threshold-based matching proportion, \varname{ProfessionContentMatch\_mean}. The two matching measures are estimated in separate specifications. Topic-category coefficients are interpreted relative to Home Design \& Decoration, as in the Level 1 models.
